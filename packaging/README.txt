XENEON TOUCH FOR macOS

1. Double-click Install.command.
2. Enable Xeneon Touch under both Input Monitoring and Accessibility in:
   System Settings > Privacy & Security
3. Reconnect the panel or log out/in if macOS does not immediately refresh HID
   ownership.

The app runs without a Dock icon and starts automatically at login.
Double-click Uninstall.command to remove it.
